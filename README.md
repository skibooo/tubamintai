# YOUTUBAMINTAI

Helping creators build, automate, and grow YouTube channels using AI.

## The AI YouTube Production Team
YOUTUBAMINTAI is a complete platform that acts as your personal YouTube manager, strategist, and video producer. Whether you are a beginner looking for a niche or an experienced creator wanting to automate your workflow, YOUTUBAMINTAI handles the heavy lifting.

## Core Workflows

### 1. Creator Mode
Stay in control of your creative process. You define the niche, topic, and style. The AI assists by generating scripts, storyboards, voiceovers, and visuals, allowing you to edit and approve every step before rendering.

### 2. AI Autopilot
Full AI automation for beginners and busy creators. Tell the AI your goal, and it will discover a profitable niche, build a channel blueprint, schedule content, and automatically run the entire media pipeline (Script -> Audio -> Visuals -> Edit -> SEO). **It never uploads without your final approval.**

### 3. AI Chatbot Assistant
A deeply integrated intelligence layer that understands natural language. Ask it to "Create a luxury car channel," and it will trigger the required platform modules, guide you through the process, and execute the backend automation jobs.
